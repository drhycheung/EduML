EduML — Learning Analytics Demo

Enter a student’s engagement indicators, absence band and semester to obtain a predicted performance level and a three-band performance class (Low / Medium / High), using linear and logistic regression.

Sample size. The source dataset contains student records from the Kalboard 360 learning management system. records were used for modelling. All performance figures on this page are out-of-fold and averaged over repeated stratified cross-validation: the data were divided into folds, repeated over seeds. Every figure is the mean across those repeats, with a ± giving the spread, so a single lucky split cannot masquerade as a result. No record is used to score a model that was fitted on it, and each figure is shown beside the simplest possible baseline.
